How it works

Three people. One network.

The citizen who reports, the volunteer who responds, and the organisation that coordinates — connected in real time.

01 · Report

Citizens report

An emergency is reported in seconds — GPS captured automatically, a photo attached, and the need tagged: water, medical, evacuation.
02 · Respond

Volunteers respond

Volunteers see only alerts matching their skills and district, claim an incident, and check in every fifteen minutes — silence escalates it automatically.
03 · Coordinate

Organisations coordinate

Every incident, photo and resource need is grouped by district on a live command dashboard, with a 🔥 risk heatmap showing high-risk zones — 25 water requests in Kandy, not 25 scattered messages.

Safety

Know what to do before help arrives.

The first minutes of any emergency belong to you. These basics save lives.

Flood
  • Move to higher ground immediately — never wait for water to rise
  • Avoid walking or driving through moving water
  • Switch off mains electricity if water enters your home
Fire
  • Get out, stay out — never go back inside for belongings
  • Stay low under smoke; cover your nose and mouth
  • Feel doors before opening — heat means fire behind
Medical
  • Call 1990 (Suwa Seriya) — free island-wide ambulance
  • Don't move an injured person unless in immediate danger
  • Apply firm pressure to bleeding with a clean cloth
