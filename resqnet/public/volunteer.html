Be the help that arrives first.

When an emergency happens, the people closest to it save the most lives. As a ResQNet volunteer you receive alerts matched to your skills and your district — and you decide when to respond. No obligation, no shifts. Just be ready when your community needs you.

Why volunteer

Your skills, where they matter.

Skill-matched alerts

Tell us your skills once. A flood-response volunteer never gets a fire alert — you only see what you're trained for.

02

Right in your district

Alerts are local to you. You respond to emergencies near you, where you can actually make a difference fast.

03

You're in control

See an alert, choose to respond or not. Check in as you go. No fixed hours, no pressure — help on your terms.
